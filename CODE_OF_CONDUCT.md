# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and helpful. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Discuss the work. Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate, reveal private information or repeatedly disrupt discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a ban.

Report concerns through [GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). You may also ask [@jamespeirano](https://github.com/jamespeirano) for help on the relevant thread without repeating private or harmful material.
