# Regroup and rebuild

## Right now
Move away from immediate danger. Check people, water, shelter and power.
Choose a meeting point, a backup and check-in times.

## Work together
People: record skills, needs and what can be shared.
Today: give each task an owner, deadline and materials needed first.
Log: separate observed facts, reports, assumptions and decisions.
Messages: share updates on a working local network.
Keep a paper copy of the essentials.

## Restore what matters
Repair water, sanitation, shelter and power in dependency order.
Test one repair before relying on it. Set a maintenance check.
If a computer system may be compromised, its responsible operator should isolate it, preserve records and verify it before reconnecting.

Planning guide. Sources: NIST AI Risk Management Framework; CISA StopRansomware Guide.
