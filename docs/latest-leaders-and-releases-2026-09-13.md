## What this means for JARVISS

The observed announcements support a transition toward agents doing longer tasks across software, alongside attention to inference costs and safeguards. My inference is that this could shorten the time between a capability improvement and its practical use. It does not establish 100–1000× end-to-end productivity or prove imminent superintelligence.

Keep three questions separate: how much capability improves, how quickly people deploy it, and whether oversight keeps pace. The reviewed sources do not supply a measured answer to all three.
