JARVISS

Just A Rather Very Intelligent Survival System

Offline AI for
extended outages.

Ask survival questions by text or voice, find mapped resources and walking routes, and track supplies.

Download once. Then use offline.

Internet is only needed to download the app and finish setup. After that, AI, voice, US maps and guides work on your computer without internet.

Download for Mac Apple silicon · macOS 13+ · About 340 MB Download for Windows Intel / AMD 64-bit · Windows 10+ · About 280 MB

Setup recommends a model for your computer and downloads the model, voice and maps.
16 GB memory recommended · About 35–50 GB free storage · No terminal or separate software installs.

Inside the app

Survival references, built in.

38 offline documents cover water, food, shelter, first aid, growing food and more. JARVISS gives the model relevant passages from these guides as context for your question.

  1. 1. AskYour question
  2. 2. Find contextRelevant guide passages go to the model
